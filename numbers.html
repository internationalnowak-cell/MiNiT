Bankroll management
Kelly Criterion — by the numbers

Given a 55% edge on an even-money bet, full Kelly suggests staking 10% of bankroll. In practice, fractional Kelly (25%) is used to reduce variance while preserving long-term growth.

Kelly % = (bp − q) / b — where b = odds−1, p = win prob, q = 1−p
